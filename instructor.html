Before the session

Give students the extracted folder and ask them to open index.html in a modern browser. No installation, server, account or internet connection is needed. Keep the HTML, CSS and JavaScript files together. simulation.html is the optional particle sandbox from the earlier lecture, not the source of these analytical outputs.

Assign groups of 3–4 one substrate each: glycogen, potato starch or amylose-rich starch. Each group initially predicts its five treatments. Rotate recorder, experimentalist and skeptic. At the comparison stage, use Reveal class dataset to share the remaining conditions. It preserves existing predictions and marks missing predictions honestly.

Students submit one results table and a short conclusion using Download notebook or Print / save PDF. Browser autosave is convenient but is not a submission system. Start with Clear this lab notebook when reusing a teaching computer.

Provenance: the experimental design and sample numbering come from LabManual_2023-2024_Danish.docx, Section A. These are authored qualitative scenarios, not digitized experimental results. Exact colour, relative band darkness, migration positions and conversion extent must not be marked as experimentally established facts.

15 vs 6No effect in the idealized unbranched substrate: no α1→6 targets. Real “pure amylose” material may contain residual branches or other components.

Relative resistance, specific colours and TLC band distributions are assumptions chosen for discussion, not universal experimental outcomes. Accept a different prediction if its mechanistic reasoning is sound. Ask what evidence would distinguish it from the displayed scenario.

Linkages and ends

Salivary α-amylase is endo-acting on α1→4 bonds; it does not debranch α1→6 junctions. Isoamylase cleaves α1→6 and releases chains, not glucose from the α1→4 backbone. Hydrolysis creates a new reducing end at the freed donor C1. An intact branched glucan has one reducing end and multiple nonreducing ends. Debranching restores a free C6 hydroxyl on the acceptor; that acceptor is not automatically a new nonreducing chain end.

The small molecular drawing illustrates selected bond cuts and labels ends from the remaining connectivity. It is not a complete polymer, a kinetic model or the source of TLC/iodine data.

TLC answer notes

  • The four standards have illustrative Rf values 0.83, 0.68, 0.44 and 0.20 for DP1, DP2, DP4 and DP7. These are display choices, not values reported by the manual.
  • Saliva scenarios show bands co-migrating with DP2 and DP4; the more accessible scenarios also have a DP7-position band. The intermediate band at Rf 0.55 is deliberately unassigned: there is no DP3 standard.
  • Co-migration supports, but does not uniquely prove, chemical identity. Low-mobility material has no unique DP assignment here.
  • The glucose standard is visible after charring, but the illustrative reaction lanes do not show a glucose spot. This is not evidence that real saliva preparations can never contain traces of glucose.
  • Short standards are invisible in the iodine view. Dotted reference lines remain for orientation; they are not detected spots.
  • Iodine reports suitable chain length, conformation and accessibility. Loss of blue colour is not a measurement of glucose, complete hydrolysis or total carbohydrate disappearance.

Good follow-up experiments

Accept an untreated + isoamylase condition, a graded pretreatment series with an independent gelatinization measurement, time courses with calibrated product analysis, a glucose-specific assay, or matching authentic oligosaccharide standards. The current design lacks untreated + isoamylase, so it cannot establish how heating changes the isoamylase response.

Use the images to replace, rather than validate by assumption, these authored patterns. The qualitative outputs are centralized in workshop-model.js; no fitting to measured kinetics has been performed.
