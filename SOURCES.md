# Source materials and adaptation notes

The workbook combines materials supplied by the course instructor into one chronological day. Original source dates and timetable slides are historical and have been replaced by the supplied 09:00–17:00 schedule. No date for the current teaching day is inferred.

## 11:00–12:00: theoretical exercise

Sources from the supplied `theoretical exercise` folder:

- `theoretical_exercise_slides_for-class_v1_students.pptx`: all six question groups, structure figures, seedling photos and TLC schematic.
- `theoretical_exercise_slides_for-class_v1_answers.pptx`: answer discussions and pathway labels.
- `theoretical_exercise_slides_for-class_v1.pptx`: checked for coverage against the student and answer versions.
- `theoretical_exercise_v2_with-answers-for-absalon.docx`: question wording and explanations, including the historical mutant question.

The source structure figures and seedling photos are reproduced from the supplied slides. Their original external provenance is not specified in those slides. The source worksheet credits [Microbe Notes](https://microbenotes.com/thin-layer-chromatography/) for the TLC schematic. Figures 1 and 2 reproduce the instructor-supplied synthesis and degradation diagrams. Numbered overlays link each red box to its corresponding reaction card.

All six questions appear inline, including the four parts of the seedling question and the four parts of the TLC question. Answer discussions are folded by default. No PowerPoint application or source-file download is needed to complete the exercise.

## 14:00–15:45: virtual starch-degradation lab

The design comes from `LabManual_2023-2024_Danish.docx`, Section A, “Enzymatisk hydrolyse af glukaner samt analyse af produkter”, and its afternoon TLC continuation.

The original 15 conditions, sample numbers, 60°C pretreatment, 37°C incubation and four TLC standards are retained. **Iodine colours and TLC patterns are authored qualitative teaching scenarios, not measured results.** The source manual is not redistributed. The small linkage diagram and optional particle sandbox do not generate the analytical patterns.

## 16:00–17:00: exam-style assignment

Sources from the supplied `eksamenligende opgave` folder:

- `eksamenslignende opgave uge 49.pdf`, reproduced as [the original Danish question sheet](materials/exam-questions-da.pdf).
- `eksamenslignende med svar uge 49.pdf`, reproduced as the original Danish suggested answers inside the locked exam discussion.
- `eksamens_aswer_prese.pptx` and the folder’s `theoretical_exercise_slides_for-class_v1_answers.pptx`, checked as supporting answer presentations. Both contain the same sequence of four exam-question topics.

The four questions are adapted into English inline. The approximate 46% potato and 43% maize β-amylolytic limits are given values from the assignment. They are not generated or independently verified experimental measurements in this workbook.

## Student work and access

Questions, figures, answers and the virtual lab load from the same static site. The offline ZIP contains all required local assets and the two optional original PDFs. Research links are optional further reading and require internet access.

Answers are stored only in the browser and exported by the student. No student work is uploaded to GitHub. The instructor supplies the password for the answer discussions and original answer sheet.
