How to use it

More balls, more encounters

Raise an enzyme concentration to add more moving particles. Each hexagon is a schematic glucose residue: a bond joins C1 to C4, or C1 to the C6 side group at a branch. The moving network is a simplified fragment, not an atomic structure or a kinetic prediction.

Endo-acting

α-amylase

Cleaves internal α(1→4) linkages in the main chain and branch arms, releasing malto-oligosaccharides. It cannot pass an α(1→6) branch point.

Exo-acting & debranching

β-amylase · ISA · PUL

β-amylase targets the purple-ringed α(1→4) site adjacent to a non-reducing end, releasing a two-residue β-maltose unit and exposing the next target. ISA and PUL hydrolyze dashed α(1→6) branch junctions.
